## Goal
Implement a real bounded response path for one explicitly enrolled non-production workload and practical incident/recovery runbooks.

## Work
Separate propose/approve/execute/verify roles and services. Bind approval to resource, action, policy version, expiry and case. Enforce a limited lease/budget at the receiving service. Verify new actions stop after revocation and explain remaining in-flight/bypass limitations.

## Deliverables
response service/adapter, controlled workload, approval audit, containment/recovery verification and runbooks.

## Acceptance gates
The monitored workload cannot grant its own lease, alter approvals or evade the protected path in the stated scope. Denial after expiry/revocation is observed externally. Unknown network/control state is explicit. Tests do not imply unrelated cloud agents are protected.

## Scope and limits
response/ and controlled lab adapter. No production identity revocation, mass blocking, third-party action or default autonomous response.

Provide the patch/PR, actual changed files, base/head revision or explicit lack of Git history, executed commands and test output, sources/evidence, approval state and blockers.
