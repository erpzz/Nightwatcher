# NW-10 — Multi-operator evidence federation and systemic assessment

Read MASTER_PROMPT.md and SOURCE_REGISTER.json first. This is a later expansion, not a prerequisite for the first useful pilot.

**Dependencies:** NW-09 working pilot; explicit authorization from real cooperating operators for any live exchange.

## Goal
Extend local detection into independently corroborated cross-organization investigation without inventing internet-wide coverage or amplifying duplicate claims.

## Work
Define and implement a minimized, versioned evidence-exchange contract. Authenticate peers; rotate/revoke peer keys; reject replay; retain original observation lineage across forwarding; and enforce partner-specific sharing permissions. Verify organizational independence outside the payload. Model actual visibility into critical identity, compute, software-release, connectivity and recovery systems. Keep hypotheses about widespread control separate from verified local failures.

## Deliverables
Federation schema/service; partner-enrollment and privacy controls; source-lineage graph; systemic assessment view; controlled interoperability and adversarial tests; explicit scope/coverage/recovery gaps.

## Acceptance gates
A copied report forwarded through several peers still counts as one observation. Fake or revoked peers cannot supply trusted evidence. Different signatures from one organization do not become independent witnesses. Missing partners remain unobserved. Conflicting reports remain visible. Lab peers are labeled lab peers. A multi-organization cluster cannot automatically declare a global takeover.

## Scope and limits
No access to third-party telemetry without operator authorization. No public release of raw incident evidence, private identifiers or accusations. No procurement or unsolicited incident contact. Stop at a tested contract and local interoperability when real partners are unavailable; do not claim deployed federation.

## Required return
Provide source revisions, changed files, exact executed tests, peer/field authorization boundaries, data-minimization decisions and actual live/controlled/unconnected status. Identify the least-supported systemic inference and the evidence needed to strengthen or falsify it.
